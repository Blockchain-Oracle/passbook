Open the app and you have an account. There is no wallet to connect, nothing to install and
nothing to paste. The key is generated in your browser on first load, and everything runs from
it: hold and send shielded value, mail a payment with a sealed note to anyone who has registered,
chat without a chain write, earn through Vesu, swap, bridge out, bet, launch.

<Callout>
The app needs no installation. If you prefer a Home Screen icon and standalone window, the
[optional phone guide](/download) shows the Safari and Chrome steps. That shortcut is not an
account backup and does not add offline support.
</Callout>

## The part that is actually different

It is not the privacy claim. Every product in this category claims privacy.

It is that **each screen names which parties can see what, before you act** — including the
parties you did not choose. There are four of them, they are listed on
[who sees what](/docs/privacy/who-sees-what), and one of them is an auditor who holds a copy of
your viewing key forever.

<Callout type="warn">
A privacy tool that overstates what it hides is worse than none at all, because its users act on
the difference. That sentence is the reason this documentation exists in the shape it does.
</Callout>

## What this is built on

The [STRK20 pool](https://voyager.online/contract/0x040337b1af3c663e86e333bab5a4b28da8d4652a15a69beee2b677776ffe812a)
is StarkWare's, not ours — a deployed privacy pool on Starknet mainnet. strk20.run is an account, nine
surfaces and a relayer built on top of it. The distinction matters when you are deciding whom to
trust: the cryptography is theirs and audited, the product around it is ours and is not.
