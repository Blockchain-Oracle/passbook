---
title: Supply a lending market
description: Put shielded USDC to work in a Vesu market, and take it back out.
---

The product route is `/earn`; `/yield` redirects to the same page. You need shielded USDC to start
and public STRK for the fee. Earn is submitted by your own account, so a covered transaction does
not apply.

<Callout type="warn" title="Rates move, and confirmation moves real value">
Rates, utilization, liquidity, and share price are live contract reads, not promises. Supplying or
redeeming is a real self-submitted transaction whose account, market, amount, and timing are public
on the venue-facing leg. Check every review value before confirming.
</Callout>

<Steps>

<Step>

### Compare the markets first

Open **Earn**. The rail lists every Vesu USDC market with its live supply rate, its available
liquidity, and a utilization meter. Nothing here is a transaction, so read as long as you like.

Three things are worth looking at together rather than one at a time:

- **The rate.** Variable, and read from the market's own contract at the moment shown on the card.
- **Utilization.** How much of the market is lent out. High utilization is usually what pays well,
  and it is also what can make leaving harder.
- **Available now.** The unborrowed USDC sitting in the market. This is the ceiling on what anyone
  can take out today, yourself included.

The list is not sorted by rate. Sorting by rate would be the app telling you the highest number is
the best choice, and it is not.

</Step>

<Step>

### Inspect the market and its addresses

Open **Details** to see the rate, available liquidity, share price, utilization explanation, and
the deployed vToken, Vesu pool, USDC, and helper addresses. The address links are there so you can
check the exact venue rather than trusting a display name.

  src="/guides/earn-market.svg"

/>

</Step>

<Step>

### Enter the amount and read the cost

Pick a market and the panel opens on **Supply**. The quick amounts are 25/50/75/max of your actual
shielded USDC balance, so the buttons work whatever you hold.

Before you confirm, the panel shows what you would receive as an estimate, the pool fee, and the
**break-even** — how long at the current rate before the yield covers the fee in both directions. On
a small deposit that number can be months. It is there because it is the part of the decision the
rate alone hides.

  src="/guides/earn-supply.svg"
  alt="Earn supply dialog with arrows to amount, fee, break-even and continue"
  caption="4–6 — The captured account is unfunded, so unknown values stay as dashes and no transaction can be submitted."
/>

</Step>

<Step>

### Review and confirm

Review, then confirm. One transaction: the USDC leaves the pool to our helper, the helper supplies
the market, and the shares come back as a private note.

The progress sheet shows build, proof, submission, and confirmation. If it reaches
**confirmation unknown**, keep the hash and check the chain before retrying; the transaction may
already be included.

</Step>

<Step>

### Return to the position later

The position appears on **Earn** and on **Positions**, aggregated per market. You do not need this
browser to find it again — the position is the note, so a cleared browser or a new device holding
the same account rebuilds it from the chain.

While a supply is maturing the pool has not yet made the new note spendable; that is normal and the
surface says which stage it is in.

</Step>

<Step>

### Redeem shares back to shielded USDC

A **Redeem** tab appears once you hold something. It spends shares, not USDC, so the quick amounts
are percentages of your share balance and the estimate below shows the USDC that would come back.

Two things to check:

- **Redeemable now** may be less than the position is worth, if the market has lent out more than it
  currently holds. The panel shows both numbers.
- A **paused** market refuses new supply but still lets you redeem. The banner says both halves.

</Step>

<Step>

### Move between markets only as two transactions

Moving is two transactions with two fees, and the plan says so up front. After the first leg your
money is shielded USDC — you can supply the new market, or stop there and keep it.

If the first leg finishes in an unknown state, the second is held back until the chain settles the
question. Nothing retries on its own, because a redemption sent twice is a real way to lose money.

</Step>

</Steps>

The full contract and note path is in [how Earn works](/docs/how-it-works/earn).
