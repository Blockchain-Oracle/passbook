---
title: Use Markets
description: Choose a live window, inspect the public terms, set a side and carry the bearer position through review.
---

Market windows and oracle prices are live reads. The values in these captures were visible on
2 September 2026 and will not match a later visit.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Choose a window and market

Use the duration tabs, then open a market card. A card shows the current price, line state, time
remaining, seed and the two displayed outcomes. You can open the card for its tape and terms, or
press a side directly.

<GuideShot
  src="/guides/markets-board.svg"
  alt="Markets board with arrows to duration tabs, a market card and outcome buttons"
  caption="1–3 — Prices, windows and odds are live; this is a dated capture, not a quote."
  eager
/>

</Step>

<Step>

### Set the side and stake

Confirm Over or Under, enter the shielded STRK stake, and read the payout and vig shown for that
exact form state. The minimum and the available balance remain visible blockers instead of being
hidden behind a failed transaction.

<GuideShot
  src="/guides/market-bet.svg"
  alt="Bet form with arrows to side, stake, payout and continue action"
  caption="4–7 — The form proceeds to review; this capture did not place a bet."
/>

</Step>

<Step>

### Review and retain the bearer position

The review explains what becomes public before submission. The position secret is stored before
the transaction is sent because that secret is needed later to cash out, claim or refund. Follow
the result in **Positions** and keep the transaction receipt.

<Callout type="warn">
A bearer position is controlled by its secret. Do not clear the browser or discard recovery data
while a position can still be claimed.
</Callout>

</Step>

</Steps>

For the public/private split, see [Markets and Launch](/docs/how-it-works/markets-and-launch).
