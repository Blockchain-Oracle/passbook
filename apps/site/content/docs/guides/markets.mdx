---
title: Use Markets
description: Choose a live window, inspect the public terms, set a side and carry the bearer position through review.
---

Market windows and oracle prices are live reads. The values in these captures were visible on
2 September 2026 and will not match a later visit.

<Callout type="warn" title="A bet is a real bearer position">
The final confirmation spends shielded STRK. Its amount, side, timing, odds movement, commitment
and transaction submitter are public. The secret that controls the position is stored in this
browser before the transaction is sent; keep the recovery backup until the position is closed.
</Callout>

<Steps>

<Step>

### Choose a window and market

Use the duration tabs, then open a market card. A card shows the current price, line state, time
remaining, seed and the two displayed outcomes. You can open the card for its tape and terms, or
press a side directly.

A standing card whose line says **opens on first bet** is a computed window, not a market already
stored on chain. The first valid bet reads a fresh Pragma median, locks that line and moves the
house seed into the window. The contract refuses a stale or thin oracle, a late opener and an
opening stake below the displayed minimum.

<Step>

### Set the side and stake

Confirm Over or Under, enter the shielded STRK stake, and read the payout and vig shown for that
exact form state. The minimum and the available balance remain visible blockers instead of being
hidden behind a failed transaction.

<Step>

### Review and retain the bearer position

The review explains what becomes public and whether your account or the covered-transaction relayer
will submit. The position secret is stored before the transaction is sent because that secret is
needed later to cash out, claim or refund. Follow the result in **Positions** and keep the
transaction receipt.

<Callout type="warn">
A bearer position is controlled by its secret. Do not clear the browser or discard recovery data
while a position can still be claimed.
</Callout>

</Step>

<Step>

### Sell the whole position while the window is open

An eligible position can be sold back before the deadline. The quote follows the current reserve
state, so it can be above or below what you paid. The app sends the whole position with a 1% minimum
output tolerance; there is no partial cash-out.

The returned STRK lands as a fresh pool note. The secret is removed only after the transaction is
confirmed, not while confirmation is unknown.

</Step>

<Step>

### Claim after resolution, or reclaim after a void

Anyone can resolve a closed market during its five-minute window. The contract reads Pragma and
checks freshness, scale and, for standing markets, the minimum source count. A price strictly above
the line pays Over; equality pays Under.

If no valid resolution lands, anyone can void the market after ten minutes. Open bettor positions
then reclaim their recorded cash-in. The **Positions** page reads claimability by public commitment
and reveals the secret only inside the pool transaction that creates the payout note.

</Step>

</Steps>

For the exact pool, contract, oracle and recovery path, see
[Markets architecture](/docs/how-it-works/markets-and-launch#markets-architecture).
