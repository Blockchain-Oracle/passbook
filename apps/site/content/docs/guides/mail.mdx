---
title: Send mail
description: Find a registered account and send it a shielded payment with a sealed note — one transaction, posted by the pool.
---

Mail needs two registered accounts: the memo is sealed for the channel the pool keeps between
them. Register your own account first, then verify the resolved address before composing.

<Callout type="warn" title="A Mail is a real pool transaction">
The final **Send mail** button submits value, pays the live pool fee and gas, or consumes one
covered transaction. If confirmation becomes unknown, check the hash before trying again because
the first transaction may have landed.
</Callout>

<Steps>

<Step>

### Start a thread

Open **Mail** at `/mail`, press **New**, then search a claimed name or paste a Starknet address. Name
search runs against a list fetched to the browser; the relayer never learns who you looked for.
A name is a label somebody claimed, so check the resolved address before relying on it.

<GuideShot
  src="/guides/mail-new.svg"
  alt="New Mail dialog with an arrow to local recipient search"
  caption="1 — Find a registered recipient, then verify the resolved address."
  eager
/>

</Step>

<Step>

### Choose what the Mail carries

Write the message. **Amount** sets what the mail pays; leave it empty and it carries postage
(0.01 of the token), which is the smallest note the reader can find it by. The paperclip attaches
an ask for money, your voter handle, or a finished bet.

<GuideShot
  src="/guides/mail-compose.svg"
  alt="Mail composer with arrows to amount, attachment, memo and review"
  caption="2–5 — Amount and attachments are optional; the memo is not."
/>

</Step>

<Step>

### Review before anything is submitted

**Send mail** opens a review sheet. Check the resolved recipient, postage or payment amount,
message, disclosure, and covered-transaction toggle. A covered transaction changes who submits
and pays; it does not turn Mail into Chat or remove the on-chain Mailbox event.

<GuideShot
  src="/guides/mail-review.svg"
  alt="Mail review sheet with arrows to the payload, disclosure and final confirmation"
  caption="6–8 — The screenshot stops at review; no Mail was submitted."
/>

</Step>

<Step>

### Watch it land

The sheet shows the pipeline — build, prove, submit, confirm — and the thread shows the mail as
pending until the pool has posted it. Once it lands, the row carries the transaction hash and
the block, and **On chain** means this browser opened the memo against the note in that receipt.
Nothing about it is stored here: reload, or open the account on another device, and the thread
comes back from the chain.

</Step>

</Steps>

For the full path, read [how Mail works](/docs/how-it-works/mail). For the transport and recovery
differences, see [Chat and Mail side by side](/docs/how-it-works/chat#chat-and-mail-side-by-side).
