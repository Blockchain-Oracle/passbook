MessageBook console

Appends a message to the MessageBook contract on Starknet mainnet, routed through the STRK20 privacy pool by the connected wallet.

What this does and does not hide. The pool sees your transaction, not your notes.

Message bodies are sent as plaintext and emitted in a public Starknet event. Anyone can read them, permanently, and nothing on this page encrypts anything.

Your account is the public sender of every transaction here. There is no relayer in this path, so the sender of each transaction is visible on-chain like any other.
